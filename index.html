Shutter Tools

Check a film camera’s shutter speeds with an iPhone. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

TraceSlo-mo videoLoad a 240 fps clip of light through the film gate and read each firing’s open time. Best from 1 s to about 1/125. SoundAudio · photodiode or clicksRecord or load audio of a light sensor on the mic input, or of the shutter’s clicks. The light sensor is accurate to 1/4000. SimTest signalsPlay flashes, clicks or pulses of a known length, with optional built-in error, to check the other two tools.
Results within ±⅓ stop are excellent; ±½ stop is fine for negative film.
