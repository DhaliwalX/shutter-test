Measured integrates all the light in the pulse, so partly lit frames count as fractions. Within ±⅓ stop is excellent; ±½ stop is fine for negative film. Firings under 3 frames long are rough at this frame rate.
How to record a usable clip
  1. Open the camera back with no film. Set the lens wide open at infinity, or remove it.
  2. Put a steady, flicker-free light behind or in front of the lens so the gate glows.
  3. Frame the gate in Slo-Mo (240 fps). Long-press to lock focus and exposure before firing.
  4. Fire several times at one speed, well inside the recording, not in the first or last second.
  5. Send the original file: on a Mac use File › Export › Export Unmodified Original; with AirDrop turn on Options › All Photos Data. A normal share converts slo-mo to 30 fps with real-time ramps at each end.
  6. If the clip won’t load here, the browser can’t decode HEVC. Set Camera › Formats to Most Compatible and record again, or open this page in Safari.

The iPhone may expose each frame for less than 1/240 s in bright light, leaving gaps where light isn’t recorded. That makes very short pulses read slightly fast. An app that lets you set a 360° shutter angle avoids this.
